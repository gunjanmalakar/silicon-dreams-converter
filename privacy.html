Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Silicon Dreams (“we”, “us”, “our”) built this video converter to work without accounts and with as little data as possible. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Silicon Dreams Video Converter (the “Service”), why, and the choices you have.

In short: no signup, no tracking cookies, no advertising profiles, and converted files are deleted as soon as your download finishes.

1. Information we process

  • Video links you submit. The URL is used only to fetch the video’s details and perform your conversion. We do not save a history of links.
  • Temporary media files. The video is downloaded to a temporary folder on our server, converted, sent to your browser and then immediately deleted. An automatic clean-up job removes anything left over (for example after an interrupted download) within one hour.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our servers receive your IP address, browser type, and the date and time of requests. Your IP address is held in memory to apply rate limits and protect the Service from abuse. Our hosting provider may also record standard access logs.

2. What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, password or payment details. We do not use advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or sell or rent personal information.

3. How long we keep data

  • Converted files: deleted as soon as the download completes (at most one hour).
  • Rate-limit counters: kept in memory for about 10 minutes, then discarded.
  • Server access logs (if enabled by our host): up to 30 days, for security and troubleshooting, unless longer retention is required by law.

4. Why we process data

We process the limited data above to provide the conversion you request, to keep the Service secure and available, to prevent abuse, and to comply with legal obligations. Where data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply, our legal bases are the performance of the service you request and our legitimate interest in operating a secure service.

5. Cookies & local storage

We do not set tracking or advertising cookies. If you use the light/dark theme switch, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. This never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

6. Third-party services

  • YouTube and Facebook. To convert a video, our server requests it from the platform. Those platforms process that request under their own privacy policies. Video thumbnails shown in the preview are loaded directly from the platform’s image servers.
  • Google Fonts. Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests the font files.
  • Hosting provider. The Service runs on servers operated by our hosting provider, which processes data on our behalf.

If we add analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

7. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information for marketing. We may disclose information only if required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, safety and security of Silicon Dreams, our users or the public.

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, isolated temporary storage, automatic file deletion, and security headers to protect the Service. No online service can be completely secure, but we keep the data we hold to a minimum to reduce risk.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because we do not keep accounts or link history, we usually hold no data that can identify you, but you can contact us and we will respond within the time required by law.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
